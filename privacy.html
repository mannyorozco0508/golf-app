Rattle Golf

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Rattle Golf is a golf scoring app for private groups. This policy explains what it stores, why, and what it does not do. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. Rattle Golf has no accounts and asks for no email address. It stores the golf information you type in — names, scores, and the games your group is playing — so everyone in the round sees the same card. It does not sell anything, does not show ads, contains no analytics or tracking SDK, and never touches your money.

What is never collected

  • No account, password, email address or phone number
  • No location or GPS data
  • No contacts, photos, camera or microphone access
  • No advertising identifier, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDK
  • No payment details of any kind — see below

Money

Rattle Golf records the games a group agreed to play and calculates the resulting total for each player. That is arithmetic, not a transaction.

The app does not accept wagers, hold funds, process payments, or pay winnings. If money changes hands, it happens between the people involved, outside the app, using whatever they normally use. Rattle Golf never sees it and takes no share of it.

Where data is stored

Round data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database, operated by Google Cloud. Google processes it as a service provider under its own security and privacy terms.

Your dark-mode setting and saved roster stay in your browser's local storage on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

Who can see a round

A round is reached through its code. Anyone holding a round link can view that round, which is the point — you send it to your group. Anyone who can guess a code could view it too, so the practical guidance is simple: treat a round link like a group chat, and don't put anything in a player name you wouldn't want a stranger to read.

Database rules restrict what each link can change. A scorekeeper link edits only its own group's scores; a plain round link is read-only.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising. It is not shared with anyone except Google Firebase as the hosting provider, or where the law requires it.

Children

Rattle Golf is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It has no accounts, no messaging, and no profiles.

Deleting your data

  • A whole round: the organizer can use "End & Wipe Round," which deletes that round's data.
  • Device settings: clearing your browser's site data, or deleting the app, removes everything stored locally.
  • Anything else: email the address below with the round code and it will be deleted.
