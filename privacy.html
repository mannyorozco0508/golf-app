Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

HardPan is a golf scoring app for private groups — the consumer app for skins, settlement, and side action. The App Store listing may still say Rattle Golf while version 1.0.3 is in review. HardPan Tournaments is the events product. Rattle Golf LLC operates both. This policy explains what HardPan stores, why, and what it does not do. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. HardPan has no accounts and asks for no email address. It stores the golf information you type in — names, scores, and the games your group is playing — so everyone in the round sees the same card. If you search for a course online, the text you type is sent through our proxy to a course directory, only to find a published scorecard. It does not sell anything, does not show ads, contains no analytics or tracking SDK, and never touches your money.

What is never collected

  • No account, password, email address or phone number
  • No device location or GPS
  • No contacts, photos, camera or microphone access
  • No advertising identifier, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDK
  • No payment details of any kind — see below

Money

HardPan records the games a group agreed to play and calculates the resulting total for each player. That is arithmetic, not a transaction.

The app does not accept wagers, hold funds, process payments, or pay winnings. If money changes hands, it happens between the people involved, outside the app, using whatever they normally use. HardPan never sees it and takes no share of it.

Where data is stored

Round data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database, operated by Google Cloud. Google processes it as a service provider under its own security and privacy terms. Looking up a course sends the search text through our Cloudflare Worker, as described under Third-party directories.

Your dark-mode setting and saved roster stay in your browser's local storage on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

Who can see a round

Rounds are shared by link or code with the group. Anyone holding a round link can view that round, which is the point — you send it to your group. Anyone who can guess a code could view it too, so the practical guidance is simple: treat a round link like a group chat, and don't put anything in a player name you wouldn't want a stranger to read.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising. Round data is stored with Google Firebase, the hosting provider. Course search text is sent only as described under Third-party directories. We also share data where the law requires it.

Third-party directories

Online course search reaches golfcourseapi.com only through our own Cloudflare Worker. That provider receives the search text needed to find a course. We may keep that search text on our proxy for a short time so the same lookup is not repeated. Round scores and player names are not sent to golfcourseapi.

Children

HardPan is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It has no accounts, no messaging, and no profiles.

Deleting your data

  • A whole round: the organizer can use "End & Wipe Round," which deletes that round's data.
  • Device settings: clearing your browser's site data, or deleting the app, removes everything stored locally.
  • Anything else: email the address below with the round code and it will be deleted.
