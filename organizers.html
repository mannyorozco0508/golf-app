Sign-up desk

Golfers sign up from a link or a QR; you approve them into the field, tick who has paid, build the teams.

A live board on every phone

Every group posts from its own card; the board updates for everyone with the link, no account needed.

The board on the clubhouse TV

Open one link on the TV: the leaderboard rotates by flight with a QR golfers scan to follow on their phones.

Prizes by flight

Flights, gross or net, skins and KPs: the results post to the board when you say so.

A link per team

Each team gets its own card link and QR; one tap sends them all.

Set up in four questions

Course, date, format, how many: the event is live in a minute, 9 or 18 holes, one round or two.

A note on every card

Weather, a rules call, the dinner time: one message reaches every group’s card.

Photos after

Golfers send their pictures; you pick what goes on the recap.

36-hole events

Two rounds on one board, gross or net, with the combined standings and the prizes on the saved basis.
