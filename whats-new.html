What’s new

What changed in each update, newest first. Swipe each row. Tap a card to see it full screen.

New in 2.0.9

2 · swipe →

What changed in this update.

  • UNDER PAR IN RED

    Red means under.

    Every under-par number on the Receipt, the boards and the leaderboard shows in red. Even and over par stay plain. Birdies are still circles, bogeys squares.

  • ASK HARDPAN

    HardPan’s replies read the same way.

    Ask who’s winning - the under-par number inside the answer is red too. Same on the live chip and the tournament card.

New in 2.0.8

2 · swipe →

What changed in this update.

  • GREEN SLOPE

    Read the green.

    Slope from 1 m lidar, drawn smooth on the map: shading, contours and downhill arrows, with the putt read at the pin. On by default.

  • RING STEPS ASIDE

    Nothing covers the arrows.

    While the slope is drawn, the target ring steps aside. The dot stays.

  • The Home Course (DuPont, WA) is added, with its green slope.
  • The whole course’s slope saves to your phone when a round opens.
  • A round recap card for Messages or an Instagram story. Money stays off the card unless you switch it on.
  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island yardages on iPhone, with a tee-to-green bar. They turn grey and say “GPS paused” when the fix drops. Free golfers get the center number too.
  • A sponsor tee card in tournaments: who presents the hole, for a few seconds, then out of the way.
  • Easier course search: it forgives spelling and typos, and shows nearby courses first.
  • Under par in red on the GPS screen.

New in 2.0.7

4 · swipe →

What changed in this update.

  • ROUND TABS

    Card, GPS, Games, Board.

    Four tabs on top of every round. The map opens on the whole hole.

  • GREEN SLOPE

    Slope on the green.

    Shading, contours and downhill arrows on the map, with the Slope switch under Ask.

  • GREEN SIZE

    How big is the green?

    Deep and wide along your line of play, the same from the tee or the fairway.

  • TEAM CARD

    Your team card, in the app.

    Card, Board and GPS on one card. Scan your team’s QR right in HardPan.

  • Why? on the suggested fair game: the strokes, the handicaps and the game it is built on.
  • Undo on the scorecard, right after each score.
  • A Season board for your weekly group, on Home.
  • Play in this event on the card of an event you run.
  • The Home card names the course and the next hole.
