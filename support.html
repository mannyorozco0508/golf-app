Common questions

How do I get my group into a round?

Set the round up, then share the link. Each foursome gets its own scorekeeper link so one group can't overwrite another's card. Anyone can be sent the plain round link to follow along read-only.

Someone entered a wrong score. Can we fix it?

Yes. Go back to the hole and change it. Everything — leaderboard, presses, skins, settlement — recalculates from the scores, so correcting a score corrects the money.

Does the app handle the money?

No. Rattle Golf works out who owes who and shows you the list. Settling up happens between you and your group, however you normally do it. The app never takes payment and never takes a cut.

Our course isn't listed.

Use "Add it here" during setup. You can scan a scorecard with your camera or type the pars and handicap indexes in once. After that the course is saved for every future round.

Will it work with no signal?

Once a round has been opened on your phone, the app keeps working and stores your scores. They sync when you get signal back. A round opened for the very first time does need a connection.

How do I delete a round?

The organizer can use "End & Wipe Round" from the home screen. If you need something removed that you can't reach, email the address above with the round code.

Something's wrong with the math.

Please report it. Send the round code, the hole, and what you expected — that is enough to reproduce it exactly. Money bugs are treated as the highest priority.

Reporting a problem

The most useful report includes:

  • Your round code
  • What you did, and what you expected instead
  • Your device and iOS version
  • A screenshot if the screen looked wrong
