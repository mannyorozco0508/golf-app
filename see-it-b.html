This is your tournament on HardPan

Before, during and after the day: what your golfers hold, what your Desk shows, what the clubhouse TV plays. Tap any phone to see it big.

Sample event: Your Club Invitational · a 72-player shotgun at your course · the names on the screens are examples
The live board, on every phone in the field
1

Set up in minutes

Course, date, format, how many. Flights by handicap, a Ladies net flight if you want one, tee times or shotgun groups, a scorecard link and QR for every group.

  • 9 or 18 holes, one round or two
  • Scramble, best ball, shamble, individual
  • Flights, gross or net, set once
2

Check-in and join with one code

A QR at the registration table: golfers tap their own name and they are in. Your Desk and the clubhouse TV show who has arrived and who is still to come. The event code joins them in the HardPan app.

The check-in board on the clubhouse TV
3

Every golfer’s phone: GPS and scoring

Yardages to the green and the hazards on a satellite map, the green’s slope, and the group’s card, all in the app they joined with. No account needed to post a score from the group link.

COMING HardPan GPS free for the whole field on event day.

4

The live board, on every phone and the clubhouse TV

Flights, gross and net, who is leading and by how much, updating as the groups post. One link on the TV rotates by flight with a QR golfers scan to follow along. Two rounds on one board for a 36-hole event.

The clubhouse TV
A 36-hole event: two rounds on one board
5

Skins and KPs, by flight

Skins carry and settle from the scores on the board; closest-to-the-pin holes take entries on the card and you confirm the winners. The results post to the board when you say so.

COMING A foursome’s own game inside the event, seen only by them; the event board stays the event board.

6

Paper cards welcome

Some groups keep score on paper. Your scoring table photographs each card; the scanner reads it and checks every hole against the app. Red where they differ, amber where the scanner was unsure, one tap to use the whole card for a group that typed nothing.

COMING Turn in our card: a golfer in the group scans the card from their own phone at the 18th green; it waits for your OK, then goes on the board.

7

Final results and the recap

Places by flight, the skins and the KPs, posted to the board when you confirm them. Every golfer gets the recap that evening: their finish, their card, the photos you approved.

Part two  ·  For the public course

Everyday side games at your course

Your regulars already play for something on Tuesday night. HardPan keeps their game on one live board, on the phones they already carry, with no account for the friends they bring. Club night is a tournament page: the first part of this page.

A side game set up in seconds

Skins, Nassau, closest to the pin, best ball, points. Pick the game, tap Start; the scoring and the board do the rest.

Join with a code, friends join free

The regulars keep one code for the weekly game; a guest types it and is in. One group of up to 5 golfers scores free, forever.

The board, live for the group

Who has the skin, where the match stands, hole by hole, on every phone in the group. The results are ready when the last putt drops; the golfers settle up themselves.

GPS on the tee, for every golfer

Yardages to the green and the hazards on a satellite map, wind and elevation in one plays-like number, the green’s slope.
