🚀 Starting Holes

Leave this on Tee Time for a normal round where everyone starts on hole 1. Switch to Shotgun for a shotgun start — teams begin on assigned holes, all at once.

Tee Time Shotgun

Fills every hole once before doubling any of them up — edit any team's assignment below afterward if you want it different.

🚩 Flights / Divisions

Optional. Split the field into divisions — Championship, A, B, Senior, whatever you call them — and the leaderboard can be viewed by flight as well as overall. Teams you don't assign still appear in Overall.

📅 Rounds

Each round has its own course, format and scores. Setup means nobody can enter scores yet; Open means scoring is live; Closed means the result is final.

🏌️ Player Field

Every golfer in the event. Two golfers with the same name are two different people here — each keeps their own scores whatever group or flight they end up in.

⛳ Scoring Groups

Who plays together. A group gets one link and enters scores for everyone in it. A golfer belongs to exactly one group.

🏌️ Teams

Each team, its golfers and its handicap. The scoring links are on the Leaderboard tab.

🧳 Link to a Trip

If this tournament is one day of a bigger trip, link it here — it'll show up alongside the trip's regular rounds.

📋 Team Scorecard Links

Each link opens that team's card and nothing else. Send each team only their own. Anyone who has a link can score that card. Tap 📤 to get a QR code or copy the link.

📋 Group Scoring Links

Each link opens that group's card and nothing else. Send each group only their own. Anyone who has a link can score that card. In a multi-round event every round has its own links.
