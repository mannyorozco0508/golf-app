Weather

 Weather
Live wind in HardPan GPS can come from Apple Weather. Apple Weather data sources and attribution: weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html

When Apple Weather is not in use, wind comes from the U.S. National Weather Service (weather.gov), a public-domain source.
