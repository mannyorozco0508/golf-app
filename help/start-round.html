Start a round

▶ 0:05
  1. On Home, the course card shows the course you're at (or the nearest one). Tap Start round ›. Wrong course? Tap Change course.
  2. On Where are you playing? check the course, tap your tees, and pick where you start: Hole 1, Hole 10 or Shotgun.
  3. Tap Start Round. The GPS map opens on your first hole.
  4. Playing with others? Tap + Add a player, or add them later (see Add golfers).
  5. Faster: tap ✨ Just say it on Home and type the round in one line, e.g. “Me, Josh 12 and Bob 15 at Papago, skins for fun”. Check the card, then tap Start round.
