# 2.1 part D - Settle up with Venmo / Cash App: privacy and App Review DRAFTS

## Privacy page (privacy.html) - lines to add

Under "What the app stores" (after the scores / players list):

> **Venmo or Cash App username (optional).** If you add your Venmo username or Cash App
> $cashtag, it is kept on your phone and shared with the golfers in the rounds (or trip) you
> play, so they can open Venmo or Cash App to pay you. It is shared only in a round where you
> have said which golfer you are. You can change or remove it at any time on the Results page;
> removing it there removes it from that round or trip.
>
> **"Paid" ticks.** When a golfer taps "Mark paid" on a payment, the round stores which golfer
> ticked it and when, so the group sees it. Nothing else about the payment is stored.

Under "Money and Purchases" (after "The app does not hold funds, move money, process
payments, or pay winnings."):

> **Settle up links.** The Results page can open the Venmo or Cash App app with the other
> golfer's username, the amount and a note ("HardPan · <course> <date>") filled in. You
> review and confirm the payment in Venmo or Cash App; HardPan never sees whether it went
> through, never sees any account, card or bank details, and is not connected to Venmo or
> Cash App in any way.

## App Store privacy label - changes

- "User Content > Other User Content" (already declared for player names / scores): add the
  optional payment-app username. Linked to the user: No (rounds have no account). Tracking: No.
- No "Financial Info" collected: no account numbers, cards or payment history. The "paid"
  tick is a yes/no on a round, not a transaction record.
- No location (nothing in this feature reads or sends location).

## App Review note (part D)

> HardPan 2.1 adds a "Settle up" card on a finished round's Results page: friends splitting
> game results. It lists the fewest payments between the golfers and offers links that open
> Venmo or Cash App with the name, amount and a note prefilled; the golfer confirms in that
> app. **No money passes through the app**: no payment API, no deposits, no fees, no
> processing - HardPan only opens a public venmo.com / cash.app link. "Mark paid" is a shared
> tick for the group. Venmo / Cash App usernames are optional and typed by the golfer.
> To see it: open any finished round with money between golfers -> Results -> Settle up.
