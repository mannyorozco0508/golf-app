Delete your HardPan account

HardPan is made by Rattle Golf LLC. You can delete your account in the app, or ask us by email.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open HardPan and tap Sign in (or Account) on the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete account. It appears once you are signed in.
  3. Read the warning and tap Delete account again to confirm.

You need a signal: if a step can't reach our servers, nothing is deleted and the app asks you to try again.

By email

If you can't open the app, email support@rattlegolf.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my HardPan account". We'll reply within 7 days and delete within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: your email address and your account id.
  • Your saved groups (My Groups), and the co-organizer links those groups gave other people.
  • On iPhone: your notification token and notification choices.

What stays

  • Rounds. A round belongs to everyone who played it, so it is not deleted with your account: the scores stay under its game code, for the golfers who have it. An organizer can erase a round with End & Wipe Round, or email us its code.
  • Two small records with your old account id: the date you were first seen and, if you had one, a founder pass. They hold no name or email, and nobody can read them once the account is gone.
  • A daily count of course lookups made by your old account id (used to limit lookups). It holds no name or email.
  • Green positions you set while playing. They never carried your name or account.

Signing out is not the same as deleting: it only ends the session on that device, and your account stays.
