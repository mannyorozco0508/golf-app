Questions

Do my golfers need the app?

No. Registration, their team card and the leaderboard are web links. The HardPan app adds GPS on the course.

Where does the money go?

Entry fees go to your own Stripe account; HardPan’s $4 service fee is paid by the golfer on top. Card payments are marked coming until Stripe is switched on.

Can I run a shotgun start with flights?

Yes. Assign starting holes, split flights, and the board shows each flight live.
