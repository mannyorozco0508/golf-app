1. What HardPan is

HardPan is a scorekeeping and record-keeping tool for golfers playing together. It keeps score, runs the leaderboard, records the games a group has agreed to play, and works out each player's total for those games. HardPan Pro adds GPS yardages and hole maps.

What it is not. HardPan is not a gambling service, a sportsbook, a payment processor, an escrow service, or a money transmitter. It does not hold funds, move money, process payments, or pay anyone. It records what your group agrees, does the arithmetic on numbers you enter, and shows you the result.
