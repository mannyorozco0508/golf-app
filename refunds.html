Tournament entry-fee refunds

Last updated November 9, 2026

This covers entry fees paid by card for an event run on HardPan Tournaments.

Who refunds you

The organizer named on the event's sign-up page runs the event and decides its refunds. We help you reach them.

How to ask

Email support@rattlegolf.com with the event name, the name on the registration and the date you paid. Never send a card number or bank details by email.

If you can't play

Ask before the cancellation deadline shown on the event's sign-up page. What happens after that deadline is shown there too.

If the event is cancelled, rained out or moved

If the organizer cancels the event, or weather or a course closure stops it, you will be told by email or in the app. If the event is rescheduled and you can't make the new date, ask for a refund.

Charged twice or by mistake

A duplicate or mistaken charge is refunded in full. Email support with the date and amount.

When the money comes back

Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Banks usually show them in about 5 to 10 business days after the refund is sent, but timing is up to your bank and is not guaranteed.

App subscriptions

HardPan Pro, Host Pro and Trip Pass are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles those refunds under their own rules.

Nothing here limits your rights under the law where you live, or your right to dispute a charge with your card issuer.
