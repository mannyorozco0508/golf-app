Start a tournament →

Run a tournament from your phone.

  • TOURNAMENTS ▶ clip 0:04

    Run the day from your phone.

    Tournaments sit right on Home. Keep score, open the board.

  • QUICK START

    A tournament in four questions.

    Name, course, when and format. You get a join code to share.

  • JOIN CODE

    Join the tournament.

    Type the event code, find your team, and you’re in.

  • LEADERBOARD

    Every group. One board.

    Net or gross, all groups or by flight, with skins as they happen.

  • FLIGHTS

    Every flight, live.

    The leaderboard updates as groups post. Flights sorted for you.

  • TEAM QR LINKS

    Every team gets its own link.

    Scan the QR or tap the text; it opens that team’s card and nothing else.

  • MY EVENTS

    Your events, on any device.

    Sign in and the events you run are listed; or open one by its code.

  • CARD AND BOARD

    Your card and the board, one link.

    Type the hole’s score; the live leaderboard is the next tab over.

  • CONTESTS

    Closest to the pin, long drive.

    Take the par-3 suggestion or pick your holes; golfers enter their mark on the card.

  • PHOTOS

    Photos when the round is done.

    After the last hole the card asks for photos; you OK them for the recap.

  • NOTES AND PACE

    A note on every card.

    A weather hold reaches every golfer’s card at once; a pace nudge goes to one group.

  • REGISTRATION DESK

    Sign-ups and check-in, one desk.

    Who signed up, who paid, who’s in the field; correct a name on the spot.
