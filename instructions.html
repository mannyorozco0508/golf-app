📖 How GolfApp Works

A quick guide to setting up a round, picking a format, and tracking the money — built for foursomes, buddy trips, and regular groups.

🏌️ Getting Started

Every round lives behind a 4-character game code — no accounts, no sign-ups.

  1. Start New Game on the Home screen if you're setting up the round. You'll pick a course, a format, and add your players.
  2. Join Game if someone already made the room — just type in the 4-character code they sent you.
  3. Once a round is saved, everyone taps between Scorecard, Leaderboard, Action, Matches, Stats, and Settle using the nav bar at the top of every page.
  4. Your device remembers your last game — if you close the app and come back, Home will offer a Resume Game shortcut.

🔒 Groups & Scorekeeper Links

If more than 4 players are in the round, the app splits everyone into groups automatically (4 per group by default — adjustable on the Home setup screen).

📱 Group Scorekeeper Links
Each group gets its own link from the Home screen after you save the round. Send only that link to that foursome — it lets them enter scores for their own group and shows everyone else read-only.
👁️ Spectator / Invite Link
The QR code and "Copy Invite Link" button on Home give out a link where scores are always read-only. Good for a spouse, a group text, or anyone just watching along — and it's the only link you need if there's just one group of 4 or fewer.

🎮 Game Formats

Pick one as the main format for the round on the Home setup screen — this drives the Scorecard, Leaderboard, and Settle pages.

⛳ Stroke Play
Standard gross or net scoring. Lowest total wins — the format most rounds start from.
🥊 1v1 Match Play
Two players go head-to-head, hole by hole. Supports auto or manual presses.
💵 Nassau
Three bets in one — Front 9, Back 9, and overall 18 — each with its own press rule.
🏆 Ryder Cup
Team match play, Red vs Blue, with the same press logic as 1v1 Match Play.
2-Man Best Ball
Partner up — the lower of your two scores counts on every hole.
4-Man Scramble
Everyone tees off, the group plays the best ball each shot, one score per hole for the whole team.
🐺 Wolf
The Wolf rotates each hole and calls Partner, Lone Wolf, or Blind Wolf before scores go in. Points-based, with configurable multipliers.
🎯 Net Stableford
Points awarded per hole based on your score vs. par — bogeys, pars, birdies, eagles, and albatrosses all worth different points.
🥩 Skins
Lowest score on a hole wins the pot; ties carry the pot to the next hole. Gross or net.
♠️ Hi-Lo
Team format splitting points for the low ball and the high ball of the group on each hole.
🎯 Dot Game
Old-school "garbage" scoring — dots for greenies, sandies, birdies, and more, tallied per player.

🐦 Side Games

Turn these on independently of whatever main format you picked — they run alongside it and live on the Action tab.

🐦 Birdie Game
Automatic — birdies and eagles are read straight from the scores. Set a $ amount per birdie, eagles pay double.
📍 Dollar Game (KPs)
Closest-to-the-pin pot on every Par 3. Everyone chips in, whoever's marked the winner for that hole takes the pot.

⚔️ Side Matches

Found on the Matches tab. This is for action between specific players that doesn't depend on the round's main format at all.

Any 2 or 4 players, any groups
Set up a private 1v1 or 2v2 — Match Play, Nassau, or Stroke Play ($/hole and $/overall with presses) — between any players in the round, even if they're in different groups or already playing their own group's main format. Everyone can build as many of these as they want, and each one tracks and settles independently.

💸 Settling Up

📊 Stats
Full scorecard breakdown, birdie/eagle counts, and a printable PDF summary of everything — main format, side games, and Side Matches all in one place.
💰 Settle
The final money ledger for the round's main format and side games — who owes who, ready to Venmo.

Questions or something not working right? Just ask whoever set up your round.
