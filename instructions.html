📖 How HardPan Works

Two ways in. If somebody sent you a link, start at You were sent a link. If you are the one setting the round up, start at You’re setting up the round.

📱 You were sent a link

You do not need an account and you do not need to set anything up. Open the link and keep your own group’s card.

  1. Tap the link. If the round has more than one group it asks Which group are you keeping score for? — tap yours, and that is the card you keep for the rest of the day.
  2. Not playing? Tap Just watching. You see everything and you change nothing.
  3. Given a code instead of a link? On Home, open Open something else, type it into GAME CODE and tap Open. Organizer’s code. Over four golfers, pick your group.
  4. During the round you move between Card, Board, Bets, Results, Matches and Game on the nav bar at the top of the scorecard, with 🚐 Trip and 🏠 Home beside them.
⚠️ The missing-score warning
Finish Round counts the holes nobody filled in and says how many are still missing, naming the first few. It is a warning, not a wall — a round can finish with gaps, and the money is worked out from the holes that are there. It exists because a blank hole and a scratched hole look the same on a phone.
⛳ The KP question
On a hole set up for closest to the pin, the card asks Closest to the Pin and you tap whoever won it. Nobody won it is a real answer — that share goes into the skins pot instead. It is money, so it is asked rather than guessed, and it is refused while you have no signal.
📖 Game
What is being played, in plain words, on every link: the course and how many holes, the format and whether it is gross or net, the Weekly Game — buy-in, pot, which holes carry a KP and what each pays, the Net Finish places, the skins rule — who is in Flight A and B, any side matches, and the groups in tee order. Read it once on the first tee.

🏌️ You’re setting up the round

Every round lives behind a 6-character game code, and a code is all anyone needs to join. An Account is optional and does one thing: it keeps you as the organizer if you change phone or browser.

  1. Home opens on two tiles. Tap Game Day — “Set up today in seconds” — for a round today, or Road Trip for several rounds tied together across days. There is a section on trips below.
  2. Played with the same crew last week? Open Open something else and use Start from it with that round’s code: same golfers, handicaps, flights and money, in a new round. The old round is not changed.
  3. Then the wizard: course, format, players, and whatever money you are playing for.
  4. Your device remembers your last round — come back and Home offers Resume with the code on it.
👤 The Account panel
Top right of Home. Your organizer identity normally lives in this one browser, so a round set up in the app is somebody else’s round in Safari. Email yourself a sign-in link from the Account panel and finish it in the same app, and the rounds you have already set up stay yours — it links the account you already have rather than making a new one. The panel also says where your free trial or founder pass stands.
👥 The Players sheet
On the scorecard, organizer only. One row per golfer: fix a name, fix a handicap, or take somebody out mid-round. A golfer who has already posted scores is asked about first — their scores stay in the record, but no surface shows them once the row is gone, and adding the person back starts a fresh card under a new id.
✅ Who is playing
Open an existing round and the count is the first thing under the code. You mark who is in from there, and the same count shows on the Round Ready screen and on each Road Trip day card. The scorecard does not ask — by the time anyone is looking at a card, they are on the tee. It is there because Monday morning the question is not what the format is, it is who is playing.
🔗 Sharing, after you save
Over four golfers the player list splits itself into groups — 4 per group by default, and each Group row carries − and + to move a boundary. The links appear once the round is real, on the Round Ready screen — not before, because a link to a round with no players in it helps nobody. On a round of more than four golfers the app’s own line is: “Send each group only their own link — it opens that group’s card and nobody else’s.” On four or fewer it is one link: “Scorekeeper link — send this to whoever is keeping the card.” You can also send the plain round link to everybody and let each golfer pick their own group when they open it.
⛳ Adding a course
A course you add or import is shared with every future round, for everyone — once it is saved, it is in the picker for every group from then on. A second import replaces the card already stored: the confirm panel says so before you tap — “This replaces the card already stored for” that course — and nothing can delete a course once it is saved, so read the city before you confirm. The picker matches the name on the sign, with or without the Golf Club or Golf Course on the end.
🗑️ Delete round for everyone
You do not need to delete a round when you finish playing. Scores save as you enter them and the round simply ends. This control erases every score for everyone in the round, it cannot be undone, and it is there only for a round set up by mistake. It appears on the organizer’s own link, not on a group scorekeeper link, and the server refuses it outright once any score has been entered. The wording is that blunt on purpose: an earlier, gentler label read to a playing partner as “finish my card”, and a live round was wiped at the turn.
📒 Season
A running ledger across the rounds one crew plays — money, skins and closest-to-the-pin, added up over weeks rather than one day. Start one from Open something else on Home, or open an existing one with its season code. A round joins a season when you set it up from that season.

🎮 Game Formats

One of these is the round’s main format, picked in the wizard — it drives the Card, the Board and Payout Settlement. Skins, the Dot Game and the birdie game are not here: they run alongside whatever you pick, and they are in Side Games below.

⛳ Stroke Play
Low total wins, gross or net — the format most rounds start from.
⭐ Stableford
Points awarded per hole based on your score vs. par, so one bad hole does not end your day.
💵 Nassau
Front, back and overall — three bets in one round, with presses if you want them. It is a main format here, and also available as a side match between any two players.
👥 Best Ball
Best team score counts on each hole. Two-man teams.
🏌️ Scramble
Play one team ball — everybody hits, you pick the best shot and play on from there.
↕️ Hi-Lo
High and low each hole: both halves of a team count, separately.
🐺 Wolf
Partners each hole, chosen off the tee. Needs exactly four active golfers.
⚔️ Team Match
Team vs. team, one bet on the whole thing.
🏆 Ryder Cup
The classic team competition across sessions, with points rather than one bet.

🐦 Side Games

Turn these on independently of the main format — they run alongside it, and they are set up with the round’s money rather than in the format picker.

🐦 Birdie Game
Automatic — birdies and eagles are read straight from the scores. Set a $ amount per birdie, eagles pay double.
🥩 Skins
Lowest score on a hole wins the pot. Gross or net. A tied hole is simply worth nothing — no skin is won, nothing carries forward. That is the default; flip the switch from No Carry to Carry Over at setup and a tied skin rolls into the next hole instead.
🔴 Dot Game
Old-school “garbage” scoring — dots for KPs, sandies, birdies, and more, tallied per player. Set a value per dot at setup; on the course each hole carries a + DOT button on the card, and on a round with groups only the scorekeeper holding that group’s link can tap it.
🏆 The Weekly Game and the KP
One buy-in, one pot, split between closest-to-the-pin holes, net finishing places and a skins bucket. The holes that carry a KP and what each pays are set in the wizard and printed on the Game tab. On the course the card asks Closest to the Pin on those holes and somebody taps the winner.

⚔️ Side Matches

Found on the Matches tab. This is for action between specific players that doesn't depend on the round's main format at all.

Any 2 or 4 players, any groups
Set up a private 1v1 or 2v2 — Match Play, Nassau, or Stroke Play ($/hole and $/overall with presses) — between any players in the round, even if they're in different groups or already playing their own group's main format. Everyone can build as many of these as they want, and each one tracks and settles independently.
🌴 The Aloha bet
A last-hole bet on a match that is already decided: the side that is down can put one more wager on the 18th so the walk in still matters. It is money between the two sides of one match, it is agreed before the hole, and like every other bet it is refused while you have no signal.

🤝 Settling Up

🤝 Payout Settlement
The final money ledger for the round — main format, side games and side matches — who owes who, ready to Venmo. It is the Results tab. Every line it pays is itemised on the page, so you can see exactly where each figure came from, under a card headed Pay out. Tap a golfer’s line and it opens to show which game every figure came from. Nothing on it marks anybody paid: the app works out who owes who, it never holds money and it never moves any.
🧾 Receipt
One document with the money, every match and press, and the full scorecard. It is on the Results tab, and the Finish Round panel opens it too. This is the one the group keeps — send it when somebody questions a number.

📶 When the Signal Drops

Half of most courses has no bars. The app is built for that, but two behaviours surprise people.

⏳ The save banner
If something did not reach the database you'll see a small banner with a count of changes that did not go through. It states the number and nothing else — deliberately, because the right thing to do differs by case and a banner cannot know which one you're in. Re-enter the hole it names once you have signal.
🚫 Bets are refused offline, not queued
A press, a KP, a Wolf call, a new side match — anything that creates money — is refused outright while you're offline rather than saved up to send later. That is on purpose. A score can be re-entered from the card in your pocket; a $78 press struck on the 7th tee cannot be reconstructed by anybody. So the app would rather tell you now than lose it quietly. Scores themselves keep working normally.

🚐 Road Trip

Several rounds tied together — Myrtle, Bandon, a member-guest weekend. One running leaderboard, one points race, one money settlement across every day.

🔗 Two links, and they are not the same
The Share Trip Link is the one for the group — everybody can watch the standings, the money and the recap. The Organizer Link is the one that can add and remove rounds and set what counts toward the money. Keep that one private; send the other. Everyone can follow a trip, only the organizer can change it.
🏆 Cumulative Leaderboard, and the line partway down it
Totals across every linked round, matched by name. Anyone who played fewer rounds than the rest sits below a line and is not ranked — their totals are real, but a position next to them would not be. A trip total is strokes added together, so a shorter week is a smaller number, and without that line the golfer who turned up least would win.
🥇 Points Race
This is the trip competition. Each round awards points by where you finished that day on net, scaled to how many played, so missing a day costs you. Points post only once a round is fully finished — a card still in progress does not count yet.
💵 Trip Money Settlement
Nets every counted round into one “who pays who” list for the whole trip. The panel ends with a line spelling out exactly which games and bets are in that figure — read it there rather than here, so it can never drift out of step with what the app actually counts. Settle up for real over Venmo; the app is just the math.
🚫 Leaving a round out
Each round in the trip has a toggle for whether it counts toward the trip totals. Use it for a practice round, a scramble, or a day rained out halfway — the round stays scoreable and settles on its own, it just stops feeding the trip's leaderboard, money, points and awards.
📸 Trip Recap Card
A card built for a screenshot, plus Share as Text for pasting into the group chat. Both carry the standings, the settlement and the awards.

Questions or something not working right? Just ask whoever set up your round.
