⚙️ Format Settings · Step 4
Options specific to the format you picked.
Match Play Stroke Play

Match Play: won or lost hole-by-hole — whoever's up the most holes when the round ends wins the bet. Supports presses. Stroke Play: total strokes for the round, low total wins — simpler, no presses, exactly 2 players.

Net Gross

A press starts a new side match for the same bet amount, from the hole it triggers on.

🔥 Manual Press: No matter which rule you pick above, a "Press" button is always available on the scorecard so you can start a press on any hole, any time.

Applies if the wolf wins. If the wolf loses a Blind call, the field splits 3 points either way.

Carry Void
🙈 Blind Wolf: caller picks lone wolf before anyone tees off. Wolf order rotates through your player list each hole 1-4, repeating.
Net Gross
Points based on score vs. par on each hole — choose Net (score minus handicap strokes) or Gross (raw score) above. Point defaults shown are our house standard — edit any value to customize for this round.

Every other player pays whoever earns a dot. A Snake works the other way — the golfer who gets it pays everyone else.

Nobody wins the KP on a par 3 and it rolls to the next par 3, worth double. Keeps rolling until somebody wins it. If the last par 3 goes unwon, it dies unpaid.

Split Pot runs two skins games at once — half the pot on gross scores, half on net.

Carry Over No Carry

A tied skin's value moves to the next hole — whoever wins that hole wins both.
